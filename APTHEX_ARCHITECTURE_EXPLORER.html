Database Models (12 Collections)

  • institutes — code, name, type, head_email, status
  • complaints — ref_id, category, details_text, mode, status, delete_after
  • contacts — complaint_id, name, roll_no, mobile, email (identified only)
  • attachments — complaint_id, type, storage_key, mime, size_bytes
  • case_events — complaint_id, event, note, member_id
  • members — email, password_hash, role, status, totp_enabled
  • staff_sessions — token_hash, member_id, csrf_hash, expires_at
  • audit_events — action, entity, outcome, ip (hashed), detail
  • rate_limits — key, window_start, count, expires_at
  • stats_monthly — institute_code, month, received, completed, escalated
  • deletion_log — ref_id, institute_code, deleted_by, reason
  • email_outbox — dedupe_key, to, subject, kind, status, attempts
